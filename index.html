One pose, two traditions

This pose comes from Nataraja, the Lord of Dance. Dancers take it to express rhythm. In yoga, Natarajasana carries his name and builds balance. We teach both, together.

  • In dance

  • Mudra

    The raised hand gesture carries meaning and feeling.

  • Expression

    The eyes and face tell the story.

  • Rhythm

    The ankle bells mark every beat.

  • In yoga

  • Focus

    A steady gaze calms a busy mind.

  • Strength

    The core and standing leg hold you up.

  • Balance

    Standing on one foot builds steadiness, on the mat and off it.

That meeting point, where expression and discipline share one body, is what NrutyaAsana is built on.

Our philosophy

At NrutyaAsana, we believe in the sacred union of Yoga and Nrutya.

Our approach is therapeutic, holistic, and deeply rooted in ancient wisdom, yet thoughtfully designed for today's needs, empowering individuals to restore balance, awaken inner strength, and embrace complete well-being.

Through mindful movement, conscious breathing, expressive dance, and personalized therapeutic guidance, we nurture harmony of the body, mind, and spirit, creating a transformative journey toward lasting health, resilience, and inner peace.
